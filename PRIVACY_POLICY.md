# Privacy Policy — EBCDIC Viewer

**Last updated: June 2026**

## Overview

EBCDIC Viewer is a free, open source desktop application for viewing and decoding IBM mainframe EBCDIC-encoded files. This privacy policy explains what data the app collects — which is none.

## Data Collection

EBCDIC Viewer does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or usage information.

Specifically:
- No analytics or telemetry
- No crash reporting
- No usage tracking
- No account or login required
- No internet connection required or used
- Files you open are processed entirely on your local machine and never leave your device

## Third-Party Services

EBCDIC Viewer does not integrate with any third-party services.

If this policy changes in the future, the updated version will be posted at this URL.
